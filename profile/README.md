### What it does

- **Chart Analysis.** Send a screenshot of any chart and the algorithm returns a full trade plan, or tells you to wait when there is no edge.
- **My algorithm.** Tune how it trades: risk, style and the markets you follow.
- **Paper Trading.** Practise on simulated markets before real money.
- **Trade Journal and Calendar.** Log every trade and see what works.
- **Trading Academy.** Lessons, practice and an exam with a certificate.

### Free, no account needed

- [Trading Glossary](https://entrixalgo.com/glossary): 219 terms in plain English
- [Trading Strategies](https://entrixalgo.com/strategies): 43 strategies with rules, examples and the evidence
- [Trading Calculators](https://entrixalgo.com/calculators): position size, risk to reward, drawdown recovery

### For AI assistants

EntrixAlgo runs a free, read-only [MCP server](https://github.com/EntrixAlgo/entrixalgo-mcp) at `https://entrixalgo.com/mcp`. Connect Claude, Cursor or VS Code to the glossary, the strategy library and the risk calculators.
